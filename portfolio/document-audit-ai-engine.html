Executive Overview

A prominent commercial banking institution in GIFT City faced significant operational bottlenecks handling unstructured vendor invoices, loan applications, and tax clearance certificates. Full-time audit teams manually verified line items, resulting in prolonged approval turnaround times and avoidable manual processing errors.

Strict regulatory compliance rules from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) prohibited using public cloud OCR APIs due to customer PII data residency mandates. The bank required an entirely private, on-premise GPU inference cluster.

The Solution Architecture

Vassu Infotech designed and deployed a full-stack on-premise AI pipeline combining custom-trained transformer models, low-latency OCR inference servers, and high-throughput GPU compute infrastructure.

HARDWARE CLUSTER Dell Enterprise GPU Racks

Equipped with NVIDIA GPUs, dual Intel Xeon processors, high-capacity ECC memory, and NVMe SSD storage arrays in RAID-10.

AI INFERENCE ENGINE Self-Hosted AI Inference Pipeline

Trained multimodal document layout analysis models, combined with optical character recognition quantized for high-throughput execution.

MICROSERVICES STACK FastAPI, Celery & Redis Cluster

Asynchronous job queue orchestrating PDF ingestion, rasterization, optical deskewing, entity extraction, and automated cross-matching against Core Banking APIs.

DATA RESIDENCY Air-Gapped On-Premises SAN

Zero external internet egress. All models, databases, and logs remain within the bank's Tier III private data center enclave.

Measurable Business Impact

  • Turnaround Significantly Accelerated: Loan disbursement and invoice audit cycles were automated with straight-through processing for standard documents.
  • Operational Cost Savings: Substantially reduced manual verification hours, reallocating staff to customer-facing risk underwriting.
  • 100% Regulatory Audit Cleanliness: Passed full RBI information security audits with complete cryptographic verification trails on every processed invoice.
