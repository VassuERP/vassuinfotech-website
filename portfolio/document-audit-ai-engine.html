Executive Overview

A prominent commercial banking institution in GIFT City faced significant operational bottlenecks handling unstructured vendor invoices, loan applications, and tax clearance certificates. Over 40 full-time audit clerks manually verified line items, resulting in 48-hour approval turnaround times and human error rates exceeding 6.2%.

Strict regulatory compliance rules from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) prohibited using public cloud OCR APIs (e.g. AWS Textract / Google Cloud Document AI) due to customer PII data residency mandates. The bank required an entirely private, on-premise GPU inference cluster.

The Solution Architecture

Vassu Infotech designed and deployed a full-stack on-premise AI pipeline combining custom-trained transformer models, low-latency OCR inference servers, and high-throughput GPU compute infrastructure.

HARDWARE CLUSTER 2x Dell PowerEdge R750xa Racks

Equipped with 4x NVIDIA A100 80GB SXM4 GPUs, dual Intel Xeon Gold 6338 processors, 512GB ECC DDR4 RAM, and 15.36TB NVMe U.2 SSD storage array in RAID-10.

AI INFERENCE ENGINE NVIDIA Triton Inference Server

Fine-tuned LayoutLMv3 models for multimodal document layout analysis, combined with custom CRNN optical character recognition quantized to INT8 precision.

MICROSERVICES STACK FastAPI, Celery & Redis Cluster

Asynchronous job queue orchestrating PDF ingestion, rasterization, optical deskewing, entity extraction, and automated cross-matching against Core Banking APIs.

DATA RESIDENCY Air-Gapped On-Premises SAN

Zero external internet egress. All models, databases, and logs remain within the bank's Tier III private data center enclave.

Measurable Business Impact

  • Turnaround Reduced from 48h to 90s: Loan disbursement and invoice audit cycles were automated with straight-through processing (STP) for 88% of standard documents.
  • Operational Cost Savings: Reduced manual clerk verification hours by 78%, reallocating staff to customer-facing risk underwriting.
  • 100% Regulatory Audit Cleanliness: Passed full RBI information security audits with complete cryptographic verification trails on every processed invoice.
