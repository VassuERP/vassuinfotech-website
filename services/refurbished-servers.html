Our 5-Stage Re-Certification & Testing Protocol

  1. Chassis & Motherboard Physical Audit: Visual inspection for capacitor swelling, pin damage, and physical trace integrity; high-pressure ESD air de-dusting and thermal paste reapplication.
  2. Firmware & Microcode Baseline Update: Updating BIOS/UEFI, out-of-band management controllers (iDRAC9 Enterprise / iLO 5 Advanced), and RAID microcode to official OEM security baselines.
  3. Memory & CPU Stress Burn-In: 24-hour continuous MemTest86 sweeps across all registered DIMM slots plus CPU prime load cycle testing under thermal monitoring.
  4. Storage Subsystem & RAID Verification: Sector-by-sector SAS/NVMe surface verification, cache battery capacity checks, and controller parity validation under sequential I/O load.
  5. Power Supply Redundancy Simulation: Automated AC power drop simulation across dual feeds to guarantee seamless zero-millisecond power supply failover.
