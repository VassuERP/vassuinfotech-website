Core Facility Engineering Pillars

ELECTRICAL INFRASTRUCTURE 2N Dual-Bus Power Distribution

Isolated A+B power feeds, online double-conversion industrial UPS banks, automatic static transfer switches (STS), and dedicated diesel generator backup.

THERMAL CONTAINMENT Hot / Cold Aisle Containment

Precision variable-speed In-Row cooling units (PAC), raised-floor plenum ducting, and dynamic air velocity controls for high-density compute (up to 30kW per rack).

NETWORK TELEMETRY Carrier-Neutral BGP Routing

Diverse fiber entry points with redundant multi-gigabit Tier-1 transit providers, dark fiber interconnects, and zero-packet-drop Leaf-Spine topologies.

SAFETY & SURVEILLANCE FM-200 & Multi-Factor Biometrics

Clean-agent gas fire suppression (FM-200 / Novec 1230), VESDA early smoke detection, 24/7 thermal CCTV analytics, and biometric anti-tailgating access.
