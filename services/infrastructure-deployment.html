4-Stage Deployment Methodology

PHASE 01 Site Audit & Power CAD

Comprehensive power load calculation, thermal airflow mapping, structural floor loading checks, and Bill of Materials (BOM) design.

PHASE 02 Cabinet & Cable Trunking

Installation of 42U server racks, overhead ladder racks, under-floor cable trays, and high-density OM4 multi-mode fiber backbones.

PHASE 03 Hardware Mounting & SAN

Server node racking, redundant PDU connectivity, hardware RAID array setup, L2/L3 switch trunking, and firewall perimeter configuration.

PHASE 04 Burn-in Testing & Sign-off

48-hour hardware stress testing, Fluke cable certification, failover simulation, telemetry sensor calibration, and as-built handover documentation.
