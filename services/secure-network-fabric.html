Secure Enterprise Network Fabric & SD-WAN

Engineer fault-tolerant enterprise campus and datacenter networking with high-speed fiber backbones, Fortinet/Sophos SD-WAN multi-link bonding, and 802.1X Zero-Trust segmentation.

High-Throughput Enterprise Switching & Routing

Vassu Infotech designs, deploys, and manages carrier-grade network fabrics for financial hubs in GIFT City, corporate headquarters, and high-density industrial campuses across Gujarat. We eliminate network bottlenecks with non-blocking 10G/40G/100G Leaf-Spine topologies.

Every network deployment integrates Next-Generation Firewalls (NGFW) with automated SD-WAN failover across multiple ISP leased lines, hardware-enforced micro-segmentation, and enterprise Wi-Fi 6/6E coverage.

Zero-Trust & Network Security Standards

  • Micro-Segmentation: Strict VLAN isolation separating ERP database servers, VoIP handsets, IoT cameras, and guest devices.
  • 802.1X Port Authentication: Dynamic RADIUS MAC and certificate checks preventing rogue devices from connecting to physical wall ports.
  • High-Density Structured Cabling: Cat6A/Cat7 copper and Single-Mode OS2 fiber backbone terminated in OTDR-certified racks.
  • 24/7 SNMP & NetFlow Telemetry: Real-time bandwidth tracking, packet loss alarms, and rogue DHCP/DNS suppression.
