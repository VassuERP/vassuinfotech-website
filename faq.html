Technical Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions & SLA Guidelines

Clear technical answers on server rental lead times, GPU cluster specifications, 4-hour hardware replacement SLAs, and enterprise infrastructure deployments.

Knowledge Base // 08 Key Answers

How fast can a bare-metal server rental be delivered in Ahmedabad?

+
For standard configurations (Dell, HPE, Lenovo enterprise servers), server provisioning and rack mounting in our Ahmedabad datacenter takes less than 24 hours via our Server Rentals Ahmedabad service. On-premise physical delivery and installation at your office premises takes 24 to 48 hours with turnkey Infrastructure Deployment.

Do you provide GPU servers for AI training and LLM inference?

+
Yes. We offer high-density GPU Server Rentals for AI configured with high-bandwidth 80GB GPUs, professional workstation GPUs, and tensor compute accelerators with NVLink high-speed interconnects, CUDA toolkits, and PyTorch pre-installed for immediate AI/ML workloads.

What are the terms of your 4-hour Hardware SLA guarantee?

+
Our Server AMC & Maintenance Contracts and hardware leases guarantee that if a physical component (HDD, SSD, RAM, PSU, Fan, or Motherboard) fails, a certified engineer will be on-site with genuine replacement hardware within 4 hours across Ahmedabad. Full terms are outlined in our Compliance & Security Standards.

What server brands and hardware specifications do you offer?

+
We supply and maintain enterprise server hardware from Dell, HPE, and Lenovo in 1U, 2U, and 4U rack chassis through our Hardware Supply & Procurement division. Configurations range from dual Intel Xeon and AMD EPYC processors to up to 1.5TB DDR4/DDR5 ECC memory, enterprise NVMe U.2 arrays, and hardware RAID controllers.

Can I rent a server for a short-term project or proof-of-concept?

+
Yes. We support flexible rental contracts ranging from 1-month trial and event leases to multi-year enterprise agreements with pre-scheduled hardware refresh cycles and rent-to-own options. Speak to our team via Contact Us for custom lease timelines.

Do you provide cloud migration, Kubernetes, and DevOps support?

+
Yes. Our cloud engineering division handles zero-downtime hybrid cloud migrations (AWS, Azure, GCP), Terraform Infrastructure as Code (IaC), Kubernetes cluster orchestration, and automated CI/CD pipeline deployment under Cloud Migration & DevOps and Enterprise Virtualization.

What is your server rental pricing model?

+
Pricing is fully transparent and determined by your required compute resources (CPU cores, RAM size, NVMe storage, and GPU acceleration) and lease term. All leases include 24/7 technical monitoring and hardware replacement SLA. Request a Custom Proposal for immediate pricing.

Which geographic locations do you support?

+
Our primary headquarters and central warehouse are located in Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, with direct field engineer dispatch across GIFT City, Sanand, Gandhinagar, and Vadodara (as shown in our Fintech Virtualization Case Study), supplemented by regional hubs in Navi Mumbai and Bengaluru.
